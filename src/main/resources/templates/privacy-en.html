Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · AI-translated from the Korean version. If they differ, the Korean version prevails.

The Mirattic account (auth.mirattic.com, the “account service”) provides one sign-in for Mirattic Sync and Mirattic Flow. It is run by an individual and handles only the information that signing in requires. The information each product handles is described in that product’s own privacy policy.

1. What we handle and how we collect it

  • Signing up with email — email, name (display name) and password. The password is stored only as an irreversible hash (BCrypt).
  • Signing in with Kakao, Naver or Google — the member number that service assigns, the nickname (name), and the email (only if you agree; for Google, only an email Google has verified). We never receive your Kakao, Naver or Google password. The nickname is used as your name when you first sign up, and you can use the service without agreeing to share your email.
  • Information created while you use the service — a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and for that session the type of browser and operating system and the sign-in time (“Signed-in devices” on the account page); the account’s security activity log (the kind and time of sign-ins, wrong-password attempts, password changes, sign-in methods connected or removed, and device sign-outs, with the browser and operating system type; IP addresses are not recorded); the sign-in tokens issued to each product (stored only as hashes); password reset request records (hashes); and a signup network record to prevent abuse (the IP address, stored only as a hash made with a secret key that exists only on the server).

We use no advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not handle location or payment information.

2. Purposes

  • Verifying who you are, signing you in and keeping you signed in (SSO: signing in once means you do not have to sign in again to Mirattic products)
  • Telling Mirattic Sync and Mirattic Flow who has signed in (see 3 below)
  • Sending password reset emails, preventing fraudulent signups and brute-force sign-in attempts, checking for account theft (security activity log), and answering inquiries

3. Provision to third parties

When you sign in to Mirattic Sync or Mirattic Flow, we pass that product your Mirattic account ID and the name and email you agreed to share. Both products are run by the same operator as the account service. We provide nothing to any other third party.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Account information: until you delete your account, then deleted without delay. Only the randomly generated account ID is kept so that it is never reused.
  • Security activity log: deleted after 90 days, and immediately when you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: end after 30 minutes without use. Product sign-in tokens: 15 minutes (access) and 30 days (refresh); each is deleted one day after it expires.
  • Password reset records: expire after 30 minutes and are deleted one day later. Signup network records: deleted one day after the last signup (cleaned up hourly).
  • Database backups: made daily, kept only on the production server for 7 days, then deleted (a deleted account disappears from backups within 8 days at most).

6. Your rights

After signing in you can change your name, password, sign-in methods (Kakao, Naver or Google connections) and signed-in devices on the account page, and you can delete your account there. Deleting your account also deletes your data in Mirattic Sync and Mirattic Flow. For anything else — access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing — contact us below and we will handle it after verifying your identity.

7. Cookies

We use only the one session cookie needed to keep you signed in (SESSION), plus a cookie named lang that remembers the language you chose. Scripts cannot read them (HttpOnly) and they are sent only over HTTPS. Clearing cookies in your browser signs you out.

8. Security measures

  • Passwords, sign-in tokens and reset tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • All communication is encrypted with HTTPS, and the database cannot be reached from outside.
  • Sign-in, signup and password reset attempts are rate-limited.
